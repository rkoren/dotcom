Season accuracy vs KenPom

Predictions are walk-forward — leave-one-season-out out-of-fold for past seasons and a held-out season for the most recent, so no game is scored by a model that saw it. “Visitor @ Home” means the venue is known — that is tonight’s board. “A vs B” means it is not: archived games are ordered by team id because the predictions log carries no home/away flag, so no home-court framing is implied. Either way margins, P(win) and the gap are relative to the home team where there is one, and otherwise to the first team named. Per-team scores are derived from the predicted margin and total, so they reconstruct the line rather than being predicted directly.
